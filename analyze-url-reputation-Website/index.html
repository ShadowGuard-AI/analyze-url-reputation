The Quarantine Anchorage

Checks one URL against the URLhaus threat feed and, when the operator sets a VIRUSTOTAL_API_KEY environment variable, against VirusTotal, and writes the result to a CSV report. One arrival is held outside the gate until its name has been run against the port's standing lists, and it is not called clean when the lists themselves cannot be reached.

Match types

The feed is parsed as CSV and the URL is compared against the url field of each listed row. An exact match, confidence high, means the URL is equal to a listed URL. A substring match, confidence low, means the URL and a listed URL overlap only in part, one containing the other; the listed URL that overlapped is recorded as matched_url. Its requirements, from requirements.txt, are pandas and requests.

Check status

The report records feed_fetch_succeeded, virustotal_checked, and check_status. check_status is checked when at least one source answered, meaning a feed was fetched or a VirusTotal result came back.

Stated limits

No entry in threat_feed_matches means no listed URL matched exactly or in part, in a feed that was fetched. A prefix of a listed URL, or a bare domain root of one, is a substring match, not an exact match. When every feed fetch fails and no VirusTotal result is obtained, the URL was checked against nothing: the report records check_status as not_checked and the run exits 1.
