One Query on the Desk

analyze-url-reputation checks one URL against the URLhaus threat feed and, when the operator sets a VIRUSTOTAL_API_KEY environment variable, against VirusTotal, and writes the result to a CSV report. The source files the work under data analysis and reporting.

One URL per check. The panel stresses the singular in its own sentence, because the tool's whole discipline is one entry consulted against a collection.

Exact and In Part

The feed is parsed as CSV and the URL is compared against the url field of each listed row. Two findings can come back, and each has its own card here.

Exact

An exact match carries confidence high and means the URL is equal to a listed URL.

In Part

A substring match carries confidence low and means the URL and a listed URL overlap only in part, one containing the other. A prefix of a listed URL, or a bare domain root of one, counts as substring and not as exact, and the listed URL which overlapped is recorded as matched_url.

No entry in threat_feed_matches means no listed URL matched exactly or in part, in a feed that was fetched. The qualification stays inside the sentence, where the source keeps it.

Was Anyone There to Answer

The report records feed_fetch_succeeded, virustotal_checked, and check_status. Check_status is checked when at least one source answered, meaning a feed was fetched or a VirusTotal result came back.

Checked Against Nothing

When every feed fetch fails and no VirusTotal result is obtained, the URL was checked against nothing, the report records check_status as not_checked, and the run exits 1.
